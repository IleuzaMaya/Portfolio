# Ileuza Maya

**Business Systems · Operations · AI-Assisted Product Development**

I turn real operational problems into structured processes, business rules and digital systems. My work combines practical business knowledge, systems thinking and AI-assisted development, with an emphasis on understanding the operational reality before choosing the technical solution.

This portfolio presents selected work in business systems, digital asset management and organizational architecture.

## Selected work

### 01 · Artline Business System — Working prototype

Recovery and evolution of a real management and quotation system for the framing domain.

The project contains operational rules for customers, products, permissions, framing composition and specialized quotation calculations. It originated from the recovery of an earlier operational system and is currently preserved in a private technical repository while its future restructuring is planned.

**What this work demonstrates**
- analysis and recovery of a legacy operational system;
- translation of domain knowledge into explicit business rules;
- investigation of architectural, routing, authentication and persistence inconsistencies;
- AI-assisted development and technical review;
- preservation of operational knowledge before modernization.

**Status:** preserved working prototype · future restructuring planned.

### 02 · Artline Image Management — Technical project

Image-management system designed to support upload, cataloging and technical organization of digital image assets.

The project works with metadata and operational information including dimensions, DPI, color profiles, hashes and predominant colors, creating a structured basis for image ingestion and catalog management.

**What this work demonstrates**
- digital asset organization;
- metadata-oriented workflows;
- image ingestion and cataloging;
- translation of operational requirements into application behavior.

**Status:** technical project · future development planned.

### 03 · Adaptive Business Engine (ABE) — Architecture & research

The **Adaptive Business Engine (ABE)** is an organizational architecture under research and validation for structuring cooperation between humans, AI and organizational systems while preserving distinctions involving authority, evidence, context, memory, state and responsibility.

The ABE emerged during the recovery and restructuring of real operational systems, when recurring problems began to require decisions about organizational meaning and structure before implementation itself.

Publication 001 exposes a deliberately bounded public view of the architecture. Internal research, governance material, private repositories and non-public artifacts remain outside that publication perimeter.

**Public technical publication:**  
[Adaptive Business Engine — Publication 001](https://github.com/AdaptiveBusinessEngine/abe-publication-001)

**Status:** Publication 001 public · research and validation continue.

## Professional approach

My work has developed through practical contact with operations and technology rather than through a single predefined product path. Across different projects, the recurring method is to:

- understand the real process before selecting technology;
- distinguish assumptions from evidence;
- translate operational knowledge into explicit rules and structures;
- investigate inconsistencies instead of masking them with local fixes;
- use AI for exploration, review, implementation and documentation while retaining human decision responsibility;
- preserve context and decisions so work remains reviewable and continuable;
- prefer understandable structures over unnecessary complexity.

## From operational systems to architecture

The Artline systems preceded the ABE.

During recovery and restructuring work, questions that initially appeared to be software problems increasingly involved rules, context, authority, evidence, memory and organizational state. Those questions contributed to the later formulation of the ABE as a separate architecture and research project.

This portfolio preserves that chronology without presenting the earlier Artline systems as implementations of an architecture that had not yet been formulated.

## AI-assisted development

AI tools have assisted exploration, comparison, review, documentation and implementation across these projects. I remain responsible for defining the problem, evaluating proposals, establishing acceptance criteria and deciding what becomes part of the work.

## Current direction

Future use of ABE concepts in the Artline systems will be described as an ABE implementation only when those concepts have been explicitly mapped, implemented and evidenced.
