# Ileuza Maya

**Business Systems · Operations · AI-Assisted Product Development**

I work at the intersection of real operational problems, business processes, systems and AI-assisted product development. My projects begin with understanding the problem and its context before deciding what should be implemented.

This portfolio presents selected work in business systems, digital asset management and organizational architecture.

## Selected work

### Artline Business System — Working prototype

Recovery and evolution of a real business management and quotation system for the framing domain.

The project includes business rules for quotations, customers, products, permissions, framing composition and specialized calculations. Its current technical repository is private while the system is being preserved and prepared for future restructuring.

**What this work demonstrates:**
- recovery and analysis of a legacy operational system;
- translation of domain knowledge into business rules;
- identification of architectural and data inconsistencies;
- iterative restructuring with AI-assisted development;
- preservation of working knowledge before modernization.

**Status:** preserved working prototype; future restructuring planned.

### Artline Image Management — Technical project

Image-management system developed to support upload, cataloging and technical organization of digital image assets.

The project works with image metadata and operational information such as dimensions, DPI, color profiles, hashes and predominant colors, providing a structured basis for image cataloging and management.

**What this work demonstrates:**
- digital asset organization;
- metadata-oriented workflows;
- image ingestion and cataloging;
- integration of operational needs with application development.

**Status:** technical project; future development planned.

### Adaptive Business Engine (ABE) — Architecture & research

The **Adaptive Business Engine (ABE)** is an organizational architecture under research and validation for structuring cooperation between humans, AI and organizational systems while preserving authority, evidence, context, memory, state and responsibility.

The ABE emerged during the reconstruction and restructuring of real operational systems, when the problem expanded beyond repairing software into understanding what should exist before implementation and how organizational decisions could remain explicit, traceable and corrigible.

The public material deliberately represents only a controlled publication perimeter. Internal research, governance material and non-public project artifacts remain outside that perimeter.

**Public technical publication:**
https://github.com/AdaptiveBusinessEngine/abe-publication-001

**Status:** architecture published under a controlled first-publication perimeter; research and validation continue.

## How I work

My work is driven by a few recurring practices:

- understand the operational reality before choosing the technology;
- separate assumptions from evidence;
- convert domain knowledge into explicit rules and structures;
- use AI as a collaborative tool for exploration, review, implementation and documentation while retaining human decision authority;
- preserve decisions and context so that work can be reviewed and continued;
- challenge solutions instead of treating the first plausible answer as final;
- prefer a smaller, understandable structure over unnecessary complexity.

## Project evolution

The projects in this portfolio are related by a practical evolution rather than by a pre-planned product roadmap.

Operational tools and systems preceded the ABE. During recovery and restructuring work, recurring questions about rules, context, authority, evidence, memory and implementation became architectural questions. Those questions contributed to the development of the ABE as a separate research and architecture project.

The historical record distinguishes this lineage from any claim that the ABE existed before its actual architectural formulation.

## AI-assisted development

AI tools have participated in exploration, review, organization, documentation and implementation across these projects. Human responsibility remains with the person defining the problem, selecting or rejecting proposals, establishing acceptance criteria and deciding what becomes part of the work.

## Current direction

Current priorities are:

1. preserve and restructure the Artline Business System;
2. continue development of Artline Image Management;
3. test and validate the ABE against concrete cases and conventional alternatives;
4. evaluate future implementation of ABE concepts in the Artline systems only when those concepts are explicitly mapped and implemented.

The Artline systems are therefore **not currently presented as implementations of the ABE**.
